01 / My story

From moving materials
to improving systems.

An investigative mindset.
An eye for quality.
A hands-on approach.

My background in supply chain management spans health and safety, quality auditing, continuous improvement, logistics, inventory management, sales, customer service, purchasing and human resources. This gives me a practical understanding of how people, processes and daily operational demands connect. I have progressed from coordinating materials and shipments to auditing management systems, improving procedures and building tools that help employees work safely and consistently, bringing an eye for quality throughout the process.

My work includes developing policies and procedures, violence and harassment prevention, employee training, chairing the Joint Health and Safety Committee, and preparing meetings and documentation. I personally developed and implemented digitally distributed forms for machine inspections, field-level hazard assessments and job completions, alongside confidential reporting and investigation processes.

I bring an investigative mindset, attention to detail and a hands-on approach to improvement. I integrate Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, SharePoint and AI-assisted tools to connect documentation, employee access, training records and company-wide notifications based on duties and deadlines. This makes information easier to manage, reduces administrative friction and helps teams identify gaps, follow through on actions and sustain continuous improvement. It also improves auditor access and visibility, creating opportunities to establish key performance indicators for information that was previously difficult to measure through traditional paper-based documentation.

I continually expand my knowledge of emerging AI tools and automation methods, exploring practical ways to strengthen health, safety, quality and data management systems while making everyday processes simpler. My AASP Quality Health and Safety Officer designation and Internal Auditor training are both in progress and nearing completion. I expect to complete both within the next four months.
